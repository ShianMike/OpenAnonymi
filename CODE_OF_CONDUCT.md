# Code of conduct

Be considerate, clear, and constructive. Participation is open regardless of
background, identity, experience, disability, or personal characteristics.

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully. Criticism should address the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private messages,
  account details, or document content without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, spam, or post
  sexualized or abusive material.
- Follow maintainer moderation decisions and GitHub's community policies.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other official project spaces, including public representation of the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close conversations, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should
be proportionate to the conduct and repeated behavior.

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the repository maintainer
[@ShianMike](https://github.com/ShianMike) through an available private profile
contact method. For confidential abuse or when no private contact is available,
use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not expose someone else's personal information in a public report.
For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
